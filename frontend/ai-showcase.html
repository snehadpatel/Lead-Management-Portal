📋 Experiment Provenance, Datasets & Limitations

Full audit log of data splits, hyperparameters, framework versions, and honest engineering trade-offs.

📦 Dataset Specifications

  • Source: Kaggle Financial CRM Lead Conversion Dataset
  • Spark Parquet Path: artifacts/cleaned_parquet/lead_scoring_clean/
  • Partitioning: converted=0 and converted=1
  • Total Samples: 1,900 instances (Stratified 80% Train, 20% Holdout Test)
  • Preprocessed Dimensions: 5 continuous + 9 categorical = 91 one-hot features

⚙️ Model Hyperparameters

  • Random Forest: 200 trees, max_depth=12, class_weight='balanced', seed=42
  • LeadMLP: Linear(91→64) → BN → ReLU → Dropout(0.3) → Linear(64→32) → BN → ReLU → Dropout(0.2) → Linear(32→1)
  • Loss & Optimizer: BCEWithLogitsLoss with positive class weighting; AdamW (lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-4)
  • Learning Rate Schedule: ReduceLROnPlateau (factor=0.5, patience=5)

⚠️ Honest Audit & Limitations

  • Decision Trees vs. Neural Networks on Tabular Data: Random Forest maintains a higher ROC-AUC (0.933 vs 0.916) due to orthogonal axis-aligned splits on discrete one-hot categories.
  • Latency Superiority: PyTorch LeadMLP achieves 0.048 ms inference latency (~103x faster than Random Forest's 4.95 ms), making it vastly superior for high-throughput streaming pipelines.
  • Non-Linear Interactions: MLP learns joint cross-layer continuous embeddings between visit duration and profile scores, whereas Random Forest isolates splits hierarchically.
